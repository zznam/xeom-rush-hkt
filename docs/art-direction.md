# Toon art direction

These three original raster assets were generated with the built-in image generation tool, then integrated into the game. No external image API or artist imitation was used. Sprite alpha channels were verified; the renderer provides a drawn fallback while images load.

| Asset                                         | Use                             |
| --------------------------------------------- | ------------------------------- |
| `apps/client/public/art/saigon-rush-hero.png` | Responsive lobby illustration   |
| `apps/client/public/art/scooter-rider.png`    | Rotating top-down driver sprite |
| `apps/client/public/art/waving-passenger.png` | Waving passenger with tier ring |

The matching code art adds pastel rooftops, striped awnings, trees, road markings, passenger bobbing, pickup feedback and delivery confetti. Sound and reduced-motion controls are remembered.

## Generation prompts

### Lobby hero

Use case: stylized-concept. Asset type: finished hero illustration for the actual browser game Xe Om Rush. Create a wide 1536x1024 polished 2D cartoon illustration of a joyful Vietnamese motorbike taxi ride through a playful sunny Saigon neighborhood. A friendly helmet-wearing Vietnamese driver on a mint teal scooter and a smiling passenger, in the right half of the scene, with small pastel yellow shop houses, striped market awnings, tropical trees, bunting, and a modest clock-tower market silhouette in the distance. Bold warm navy outlines, hand-drawn rounded shapes, flat cel shading, warm cream, tangerine, coral, teal and leafy green, subtle paper grain, expressive and charming, premium indie game key art. Perspective three-quarter side view for this hero only, lively motion marks and small sparkles. Composition: leave the left 40 percent simpler with warm pale cream sky and road for live HTML heading and button overlays; characters grouped on right center with breathable margins, full scooter visible. Bright daytime, playful and inviting. No text, no letters, no logos, no watermark, no interface, no dark cyberpunk styling. This image will be a background cropped responsively, keep principal characters in the right-center safe area.

### Scooter rider

Use case: stylized-concept. Asset type: one production game sprite, transparent PNG, square 1024x1024. A single cheerful mint-teal Vietnamese scooter with one helmet-wearing rider, seen from STRICT DIRECTLY OVERHEAD TOP DOWN 90 degree orthographic camera, pointing to the RIGHT (east). Entire scooter and handlebars visible, bike long axis perfectly horizontal. Cute chunky rounded 2D indie cartoon style, thick dark forest-navy outline, flat cel shading, teal scooter body, warm cream helmet, coral shirt sleeve details, small black wheels. This must read clearly when scaled down to 64x64 pixels. The rider's helmet is seen from above; no front portrait face. Center the complete vehicle on canvas, occupy 85 percent of width and about 50 percent of height; ample transparent padding around all edges. Actual transparent alpha background, no ground, no floor, no sky, no text, no watermark, no multiple sprites, no sprite sheet, no side-view or isometric perspective. Minimal small contact shadow only beneath scooter.

### Passenger

Use case: stylized-concept. Asset type: transparent PNG passenger character for Xe Om Rush, a cute Vietnamese motorbike taxi browser game. Create ONE friendly Vietnamese passenger character, full body, waving one hand to hail a ride. Mint-green short-sleeve shirt, warm yellow small backpack, navy shorts, cream sneakers, rounded friendly face with black hair, simple chunky proportions with large head and small body. Hand-drawn 2D cartoon with thick dark forest-navy outlines, clean flat cel shading, warm cream highlights and coral cheeks, premium casual indie game art. Near-front view with a gentle top-down tilt, clear silhouette even at 40px high. Centered complete character occupies 85 percent of height on a square 1024x1024 canvas. True transparent alpha background, no scenery, no text, no watermark, no border, no ground plane, no other characters. Match a sunny pastel Saigon scooter game, cheerful and approachable.
